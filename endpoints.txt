visual-anomaly-inspection-api — Endpoints (bottle-only)
=========================================================

Base URL (production):
  https://salmeida-bottle-anomaly-detection.hf.space

Local dev:
  http://localhost:7860

Supported category: bottle

GET /
GET /health
GET /metadata
POST /predict  (multipart: file, category=bottle, include_images, include_debug, include_overlay)
POST /infer    (legacy alias)

Exemplo:
curl -s -X POST "https://salmeida-bottle-anomaly-detection.hf.space/predict" \
  -F "category=bottle" \
  -F "include_images=false" \
  -F "file=@imagem/anomaly_1.png" | jq '.status, .scores, .boxes'
